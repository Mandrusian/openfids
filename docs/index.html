01 / THE BOARD

More than a
departure time.

A quick glance when you need the essentials. A detailed view when you want to follow every movement.

01

Follow the movement

Arrivals and departures from the public NQA feed. Filter by airport, terminal, airline or aircraft, and star flights for your watchlist.

02

Get the full picture

Aircraft types, registrations, gates, bays and carousels. Compare scheduled, estimated, actual and block times in the detailed layout.

03

Set your atmosphere

Choose your theme, tune Liquid Glass, switch between 12 and 24-hour time, or make the board compact for your screen.

↳

Your own board, on your own computer. The installed version also supports custom movements and feed adapters. Settings and custom flights stay local.

03 / MAKE IT YOURS

A permanent spot
on your desktop.

Run OpenFIDS locally with the installer for your platform. You can review the scripts and manual setup instructions on GitHub.

⌘

Linux & macOS

Run in a Bash terminal

⊞

Windows

Run in PowerShell

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mandrusian/openfids/main/install.ps1 | iex
