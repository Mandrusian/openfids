THE DETAILS MATTER

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

An information-dense airport display that still feels beautiful. Switch from a comfortable board to all the operational detail whenever you like.

✈

Real airport data

Arrivals, departures, terminals, aircraft, registrations, gate information and estimated times from North Queensland Airports.

◈

Liquid Glass, reimagined

Frosted surfaces, soft colour gradients, beautiful motion and adjustable glass depth to suit your setup.

★

Flight watchlists

Star the movements you care about, with optional chimes when flight details change.

▤

Deep flight information

Scheduled, estimated, actual and block times, codeshares, parking bays, baggage carousels and much more.

＋

Your own movements

Add custom flights and special movements locally in the installed desktop application.

⌁

Extensible and private

Use your own JSON feed or Python adapter. OpenFIDS keeps your settings and custom movements on your computer.

READY WHEN YOU ARE

Make it your own.

Install the complete local version in minutes, or read the source before running anything.

⌘

Linux and macOS

Paste into your Bash terminal

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mandrusian/openfids/main/install.sh | bash
⊞

Windows

Paste into PowerShell

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mandrusian/openfids/main/install.ps1 | iex
